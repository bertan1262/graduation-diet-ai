Slide 2 of 3 • Architecture & Innovation
📸
1. Real-Time Computer Vision & Depth Analysis
Detects multiple food classes in a single camera frame. Leverages contour volumetric heuristics to estimate portion weight without requiring physical kitchen scales.
⚡
2. Algorithmic BMR & Dynamic Planning
Calculates exact Basal Metabolic Rate via the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. Dynamically generates allergen-safe, goal-oriented (loss/gain/maintenance) weekly meal plans.
🛡️
3. Ethical Conversational AI & Guardrails
Multi-turn dietary advisor powered by Gemini API, reinforced with strict safety guardrails: strictly rejects medical diagnoses and prohibits dangerous caloric deficits.
🔬 REAL-TIME CV FOOD DETECTION & SAFETY PIPELINE
AI Model Online • 96.4% Conf.
0:35 - 1:15 SPEAKER SCRIPT
"To achieve this purpose, our engineering design focuses on three innovative technical objectives. First, our computer vision module uses the smartphone camera to recognize food items and utilizes depth-based estimation to approximate portion size, translating visual data directly into macronutrients. Second, our personalization engine calculates exact Basal Metabolic Rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to generate dynamic, allergy-safe weekly meal schedules. Third, our conversational AI leverages Gemini with strict safety guardrails—actively rejecting medical diagnostic queries to guarantee zero clinical liability."
